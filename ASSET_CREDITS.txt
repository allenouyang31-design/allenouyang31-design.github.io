GUARDIAN KNOCKDOWN / 守卫冲撞倒地
A_GuardianKnockdown_InPlace is a project-local, root-locked copy of the existing MM_Death_Back_01 sequence. The prototype samples it forward for the fall and backward for recovery, while Blueprint rotates the character mesh up to 90 degrees and applies up to 35 cm of vertical compensation to make the downed pose visible above the floor. The shared source animation is unchanged. This is a reused and procedurally adapted animation, not a newly authored fall/get-up sequence or new motion capture. The exact package originally imported into the project remains unrecorded.
A_GuardianKnockdown_InPlace是已有MM_Death_Back_01的项目内锁根副本。原型正向采样表现跌倒、反向采样恢复，同时由蓝图将人物网格倾倒最多90度并提供最多35厘米高度补偿，让倒地姿态清楚显示在地面之上。共享源动作未修改。这是复用与程序化适配，不是本次原创跌倒/起身动画或新增动作捕捉；原工程具体导入包仍未记录。
